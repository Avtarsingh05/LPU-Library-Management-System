Privacy Policy
1. Information We Collect

We collect basic profile information (name, email, ID) to manage your library account and track book issuances securely.

2. Data Usage

Your data is strictly used for library administration (sending due date reminders, calculating fines, and managing inventory). We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

3. Data Security

All sensitive credentials and personal data are encrypted and securely stored in Google Firebase. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

Terms of Service
1. Borrowing Rules

Members must return borrowed books on or before the due date. A maximum limit of 5 concurrent books applies to all student accounts.

2. Fines and Penalties

Overdue books incur a daily fine. Any physical damage or loss of a book will result in a manual penalty fee equivalent to the book's replacement value.

3. Account Suspension

The library administration reserves the right to suspend any account that repeatedly violates borrowing guidelines or attempts to circumvent the payment gateway.
